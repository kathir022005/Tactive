Stage 1 — The Problem

Corporate Equipment Chaos

Large organisations face a common pain point: equipment reservations managed via spreadsheets cause double-bookings, revenue loss from unreturned equipment, and wasted admin time.

🗓️
Double Bookings
No enforcement means two employees try to take the same laptop on the same day.
💸
Unreturned Equipment
No automated late fee calculation means no accountability when equipment is returned overdue.
⏱️
Manual Admin Overhead
Admins manually approve every request, no prioritisation system for high-value employees.
